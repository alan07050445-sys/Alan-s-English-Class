## Part 2 — Read and circle 10 -VCe words.（20 分）

> A -VCe word has a long vowel sound and a silent e at the end, like cake or home.

Last June a young whale swam too close to a small town. The tide went out and left the huge
animal on the wet sand. A girl named Jane saw it when she rode past on her old blue bicycle.
She ran all the way back to tell her dad, and he made a plan. All night the people of the town
worked hard to keep the animal safe. They poured sea water on its skin so the hot sun would not
burn it. When the morning sun rose, the deep water slowly washed back in. The great tail
slapped the waves, and then the sea was quiet again.

**答案（程式自己算的，不用你填）**：June, whale, close, tide, huge, Jane, rode, made, safe, rose
　→ 文章裡**剛好 10 個**，而且沒有 have / give 這種「拼法符合但母音不長」的例外字。

## Part 4 — 📦 Find the 5 capitalization mistakes. Write the corrected words in the boxes.（15 分，3 分一題）

> Days, months, names, languages and the word I always begin with a capital letter.

Last friday i went to the beach with my class. Our teacher showed us a baby sea turtle.
In october the turtles swim to warmer water. My friend amy wrote about the trip in english class.

| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
|   |   |   |   |   |

**答案**：Friday　I　October　Amy　English
- 找到的順序**不用**跟上面一樣，程式不管順序。
- **大小寫要寫對**才算對（寫 `friday` 會回「這個字對了，但大小寫不對」）。

## Part 5 — 🔍 Use two specific details from the text.（20 分，10 分一題）

Every fall, monarch butterflies leave the cold north and begin a very long journey south.
These small insects can fly more than two thousand miles to reach the warm forests of Mexico.
They do not carry a map. Scientists think the butterflies use the sun and the pull of the earth
to find the same trees their great-grandparents once used. On the way they must stop at flowers,
because nectar is the only fuel they have. When they arrive, thousands of them rest together on
one branch and keep each other warm until spring.

**How are monarch butterflies able to finish such a long journey?
Use TWO specific details from the text to explain.**

Detail 1 ______________________________________________

Detail 2 ______________________________________________

程式會驗三件事：
1. 這一句有沒有**真的從文章裡抄出證據**（連續三個字以上），自己憑印象寫的不算。
2. 兩格要用**文章裡不同的句子** —— 同一句的前半和後半算同一個細節。
3. 字數夠不夠（這份設 8 個字以上）。
答對也會印出「你引用到哪一段」，孩子才知道這題在練什麼。

## Part 6 — ✍️ Narrative Writing（25 分，老師改）

**Write a narrative about a time you helped someone, or helped an animal.
Tell what happened first, what the problem was, and how it ended.**

> 用到這幾個字：**journey　safe　huge　brave　finally**
> 寫 5 段，每段至少 4 句。

評分用**康橋寫作七項**（Content / Details / Creativity / Text Structure / Length / Grammar /
Vocabulary，每項 7 分四等第）。程式**不給分**，只在交卷前幫孩子數：
幾段、每段幾句、幾個字、用到幾個指定單字 —— 那正是 rubric 裡 Length 與 Vocabulary 數得出來的部分。
