# 貼進「✏️ 出考卷」用的文字

每一大題照著設：**題型下拉**、**標題**、**配分**（還有幾個小欄位），
然後把「內容」整塊貼進下面那個大框框。六大題都做完就是一份 100 分的卷子。

---

## Section 1：`Section 1: Before You Begin`　（0%）

- **題型**：開頭的心情與目標
- **標題**：`Part 1: Check In`
- **配分**：0（這一題不計分，填 0）
- **內容**（整塊貼進去）：

```
Read every question carefully.
Underline the key words in each question.
Check my spelling before I hand it in.
Stay calm and do my best.
```

---

## Section 2：`Section 2: Word Study`　（20%）

- **題型**：圈出符合的字
- **標題**：`Part 2: Read and circle 10 -VCe words.`
- **配分**：20
- **其他欄位**：「個」填 10
- **內容**（整塊貼進去）：

```
vce
Last June a young whale swam too close to a small town. The tide went out and left the huge animal on the wet sand. A girl named Jane saw it when she rode past on her old blue bicycle. She ran all the way back to tell her dad, and he made a plan. All night the people of the town worked hard to keep the animal safe. They poured sea water on its skin so the hot sun would not burn it. When the morning sun rose, the deep water slowly washed back in. The great tail slapped the waves, and then the sea was quiet again.
```

---

## Section 3：`Section 3: Vocabulary`　（20%）

- **題型**：Word Bank 填空
- **標題**：`Part 3: Fill in each blank with a word from the box.`
- **配分**：20
- **內容**（整塊貼進去）：

```
journey, tide, huge, safe, nectar
The butterflies make a long ___ to Mexico every fall. | journey
When the ___ went out, the whale was left on the sand. | tide
A blue whale is a ___ animal, far bigger than a bus. | huge
Butterflies drink ___ from flowers to get energy. | nectar
The town worked all night to keep the whale ___. | safe
```

---

## Section 4：`Section 4: Grammar`　（15%）

- **題型**：找出錯的字，填進格子
- **標題**：`Part 4: Find the 5 capitalization mistakes. Write the corrected words in the boxes.`
- **配分**：15
- **內容**（整塊貼進去）：

```
Friday, I, October, Amy, English

Last friday i went to the beach with my class. Our teacher showed us a baby sea turtle. In october the turtles swim to warmer water. My friend amy wrote about the trip in english class.
```

---

## Section 5：`Section 5: Reading Comprehension`　（20%）

- **題型**：用文章裡的 N 個細節回答
- **標題**：`Part 5: Use two specific details from the text.`
- **配分**：20
- **其他欄位**：「個」填 2、「字以上」填 8
- **內容**（整塊貼進去）：

```
How are monarch butterflies able to finish such a long journey? Use TWO specific details from the text to explain.

Every fall, monarch butterflies leave the cold north and begin a very long journey south. These small insects can fly more than two thousand miles to reach the warm forests of Mexico. They do not carry a map. Scientists think the butterflies use the sun and the pull of the earth to find the same trees their great-grandparents once used. On the way they must stop at flowers, because nectar is the only fuel they have. When they arrive, thousands of them rest together on one branch and keep each other warm until spring.
```

---

## Section 6：`Section 6: Writing`　（25%）

- **題型**：長文寫作（老師改）
- **標題**：`Part 6: Narrative Writing`
- **配分**：25
- **其他欄位**：「段 × 句」填 5 × 4、勾 **Rubric** → 下拉選「康橋寫作七項」
- **內容**（整塊貼進去）：

```
Write a narrative about a time you helped someone, or helped an animal. Tell what happened first, what the problem was, and how it ended.

journey, safe, huge, brave, finally
```

---

六大題都貼完之後，出卷頁右上角的合計應該顯示 **合計 100 分（自動 75／老師改 25）**，
而且警告欄是空的。對不上就是哪一格的配分打錯了。
