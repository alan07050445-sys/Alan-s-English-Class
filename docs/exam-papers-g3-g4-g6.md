同一份規格、三種難度，四個新題型（😊 心情與目標、📦 改錯格子、🔍 找細節、✍️ 長文寫作）每一份都有。

| Vocabulary | Word Bank 5 格 | Word Bank 5 格 | Word Bank 5 格 |
| 📦 Grammar | 句首大寫＋名字（5 個）| 日期／語言／名字（5 個）| 國名／地名／語言／月份（5 個）|
| 🔍 Reading | **2** 個細節，6 字以上 | **2** 個細節，8 字以上 | **3** 個細節，10 字以上 |
| ✍️ Writing | 3 段 × 3 句，三項 rubric | 5 段 × 4 句，康橋七項 | 5 段 × 4 句，康橋七項 |

### 🔵 Part 2: Read and circle 10 words with sh or ch.　**25 分**

> Two letters can work together to make one sound, like sh in shop or ch in chair.

Mia had a new fish tank by her bed. Every morning Mia put a pinch of food on top of the water. One day the water went cloudy and her pet did not swim much. Mia told her mom, who showed her how to wash the small stones with a brush. They put in fresh water and one green plant with a shiny leaf. Mia sat on the floor and waited until lunch. Then her pet came up to the top, and Mia gave a happy shout.

**答案**（程式自己算的）：fish、pinch、much、showed、wash、brush、fresh、shiny、lunch、shout

4. They ______ the honey in little rooms made of wax.　　*（store）*

## G6　Grade 6 Formative Assessment 1（100 分）

- [ ] Leave time to check my work at the end.

### 🔵 Part 2: Read and circle 10 words that end in -ment, -tion or -ity.　**15 分**

> These suffixes turn a verb or an adjective into a noun: improve → improvement.

The town council made an announcement last spring: the old playground would be closed, and no one knew when it would open again. For the students at Pine Middle School, that news felt unfair. They formed a small group and wrote a careful statement about the need for a safe place to play. Their argument was simple. Without that equipment, the younger children had no activity after school and nowhere to go. The group asked for a meeting and brought a clear solution of their own: a weekend clean-up run by the students themselves. The council paid attention. One member said the quality of the plan surprised her. By November the money was found, the repairs began, and the improvement was finished before the first snow. The whole community came to the opening.

**答案**（程式自己算的）：announcement、statement、argument、equipment、activity、solution、attention、quality、improvement、community

## 已經驗過的

- 三份都是 **100 分整**、各 Section 百分比合計 100%、出卷頁**零警告**
- 三份的 Word Study 都用網站的檢查器數過，文章裡**剛好 10 個**符合
- G6 第一版的 `city` 是**假陽性**（字尾像 -ity 但不是「字根＋ity」）→ 已換掉，
  不然學生圈了算對、沒圈扣分，等於教錯
- 三份的改錯題答案都**真的出現在那段文章裡**
- 六份模擬作答（每份全對一次、典型錯一次）：
  G3 90/90 與 57/90，G4 75/75 與 57/75，G6 75/75 與 44/75，每一項都回了看得懂的原因
